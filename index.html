ABOUT TALON

One name.
A lot of directions.

Talon is an independent creative group focused on building and sharing original projects. Games, software, stories, music, designs, and community all have a place here.

There isn’t one lane we stick to. The point is to keep making things, improve them, and bring people along for the ride.
